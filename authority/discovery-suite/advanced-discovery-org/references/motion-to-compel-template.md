# Motion to Compel Discovery & Sanctions Argument

**Rule**: Fla. Fam. L.R.P. 12.380  
**Sanctions Rule**: Rule 12.380 authorizes fees, expenses, and other sanctions for unjustified discovery failures.

---

## SANCTIONS ARGUMENT (RULE 12.380)

Use this section when escalating beyond a standard motion to compel — i.e., the party has already been ordered to produce and has not complied, or the conduct is so egregious that sanctions beyond fees are warranted.

```
ARGUMENT FOR SANCTIONS UNDER RULE 12.380

Florida Family Law Rule of Procedure 12.380 authorizes the Court to impose sanctions 
when a party fails to obey an order to provide or permit discovery, fails to serve 
answers or objections to interrogatories, or fails to respond to a request for 
production. Sanctions may include:

(a) Treating as contempt of court the failure to comply;
(b) Establishing as admitted the facts that the discovery was designed to obtain;
(c) Prohibiting the disobedient party from introducing designated matters in evidence;
(d) Striking pleadings or parts thereof;
(e) Dismissing the action or proceeding or any part thereof; and/or
(f) Requiring the disobedient party to pay reasonable expenses caused by the failure, 
including attorney's fees.

Here, [OPPOSING PARTY] has [describe the conduct: failed to comply with this Court's 
order dated [DATE] requiring production of [ITEMS] by [DATE] / repeatedly failed to 
respond to discovery despite [NUMBER] separate deficiency letters / produced documents 
in a manner designed to obscure responsive records / asserted objections that have no 
colorable basis and are being used to delay disclosure of [SPECIFIC RECORDS]].

The appropriate sanction is [describe: award of fees and costs / establishment of 
[specific facts] as admitted / preclusion of evidence / other]. [OPPOSING PARTY]'s 
conduct is not substantially justified. The prejudice to [MOVING PARTY] includes 
[describe: inability to prepare for hearing / necessity of filing multiple motions / 
incomplete financial picture at mediation].
```
