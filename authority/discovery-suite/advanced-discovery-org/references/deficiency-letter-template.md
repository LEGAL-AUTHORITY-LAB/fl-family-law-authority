# Deficiency Letter Templates

Two versions: (1) Mandatory Disclosure Deficiency Letter; (2) Advanced Discovery Meet-and-Confer / Deficiency Letter

---

## VERSION 1: MANDATORY DISCLOSURE DEFICIENCY LETTER

```
[DATE]

VIA EMAIL: [OPPOSING COUNSEL EMAIL]
[OPPOSING COUNSEL NAME], Esquire
[FIRM NAME]
[ADDRESS]

        Re:     [CLIENT NAME] v. [OPPOSING PARTY NAME]
                Case No.: [CASE NUMBER]
                Mandatory Disclosure Deficiency

Dear [OPPOSING COUNSEL]:

We are writing regarding [OPPOSING PARTY]'s obligation to serve mandatory automatic 
disclosure pursuant to Florida Family Law Rule of Procedure 12.285.

[SELECT ONE]:

[IF NOT SERVED AT ALL]:
Petitioner/Respondent filed the [Petition / Answer] in this matter on [DATE]. Mandatory 
disclosure was due no later than [DATE — 45 days from service]. To date, we have not 
received any mandatory disclosure from your client, including the financial affidavit 
required by Rule 12.285.

Please serve complete mandatory disclosure, including a completed Financial Affidavit, 
on or before [DATE — 10–14 days from this letter]. If we do not receive complete 
disclosure by that date, we will file a Motion to Compel Mandatory Disclosure and seek 
attorney's fees and costs pursuant to Rule 12.380.

[IF SERVED BUT INCOMPLETE]:
On [DATE], we received documents purporting to constitute your client's mandatory 
disclosure. Upon review, the following items required by Rule 12.285 are missing or 
deficient:

1. [SPECIFIC MISSING ITEM — e.g., "Federal and state income tax returns for [YEAR] were 
not included. Rule 12.285(b)(2) requires production of the most recently filed returns."]

2. [SPECIFIC MISSING ITEM — e.g., "Pay stubs for the three (3) months preceding service 
were not produced. Rule 12.285(b)(3) requires production of all pay stubs for the 
preceding three months."]

3. [SPECIFIC MISSING ITEM — e.g., "Bank account statements for all accounts were not 
produced. The Financial Affidavit discloses an account at [BANK] ending in [XXXX] for 
which no statements were provided."]

4. [CONTINUE AS NEEDED]

Please supplement your client's mandatory disclosure to cure the above deficiencies 
on or before [DATE — 10–14 days from this letter]. If we do not receive complete 
supplemental disclosure by that date, we will file a Motion to Compel and seek 
attorney's fees and costs.

Please note that Rule 12.285 imposes a continuing duty to supplement disclosure when 
prior information becomes inaccurate. We expect your client to comply with this 
continuing obligation throughout this proceeding.

                              Sincerely,

                              [ATTORNEY NAME], Esquire
                              FAMILY MATTERS LAW GROUP, P.A.
                              [ADDRESS]
                              [EMAIL]
                              Attorney for [CLIENT NAME]
```

```
[OPPOSING COUNSEL NAME], Esquire
[FIRM NAME]
[ADDRESS]

                Case No.: [CASE NUMBER]
                Discovery Deficiency — [RFP / Interrogatories / RFAs] Served [DATE]

Dear [OPPOSING COUNSEL]:

We are writing pursuant to Florida Family Law Rule of Procedure 12.380 and the Court's 
expectations regarding good-faith resolution of discovery disputes, to identify specific 
deficiencies in your client's responses to [PETITIONER'S / RESPONDENT'S] [First Request 
for Production / First Set of Interrogatories / First Requests for Admission] served on 
[DATE].

Responses were due on [DATE — 30 days from service]. [We received responses on [DATE]. / 
We have not received any response to date.]

The following specific deficiencies require immediate attention:

REQUEST NO. [X] — [BRIEF DESCRIPTION]:
Your client responded: "[PASTE VERBATIM RESPONSE]"
Deficiency: [DESCRIBE SPECIFICALLY — e.g., "The response objects on grounds of 
overbreadth but fails to produce any documents responsive to the non-objectionable 
portion of the request. The request is narrowly limited to bank statements for the 
specific accounts identified on your client's Financial Affidavit. There is no colorable 
basis for overbreadth. Production of the identified accounts is required."]

REQUEST NO. [X] — [BRIEF DESCRIPTION]:
Your client responded: "[PASTE VERBATIM RESPONSE]"
Deficiency: [DESCRIBE]

[CONTINUE FOR EACH DEFICIENT RESPONSE]

Please cure the above deficiencies and produce all responsive documents on or before 
[DATE — 10–14 days from this letter].

If we are unable to resolve these deficiencies without court intervention, we will file 
a Motion to Compel and will seek attorney's fees and costs under Rule 12.380. We are 
available to confer by phone before [DATE] if you believe a call would be productive.

                              Sincerely,

                              [ATTORNEY NAME], Esquire
                              FAMILY MATTERS LAW GROUP, P.A.
                              [ADDRESS]
                              [EMAIL]
                              Attorney for [CLIENT NAME]
```

## DRAFTING NOTES

- Send by email. Confirm delivery. Save to Smokeball tagged as "Discovery – Deficiency Letter."
- Use Version 1 when the problem is Rule 12.285 non-compliance — tie each deficiency to a specific subsection of the rule.
- Use Version 2 for advanced discovery deficiencies — quote the exact response received and identify exactly what is missing or objected to improperly.
- Set a firm deadline (10–14 days is standard). If the deadline passes without response, motion to compel is the next move — do not send repeated deficiency letters.
- If no response at all was ever received to discovery, note that in the letter and separately note that a deemed admission argument exists for RFAs under Rule 12.370.
