# Interrogatories Template & Request for Admissions Template

---

## INTERROGATORIES — TEMPLATE

**Rule**: Fla. Fam. L.R.P. 12.340  
**Response deadline**: 30 days from service  
**Limit**: Standard family law interrogatories; additional interrogatories require court permission or stipulation  

```
[CAPTION — same as RFP template]

[PETITIONER'S / RESPONDENT'S] FIRST SET OF INTERROGATORIES TO [OPPOSING PARTY]

Pursuant to Florida Family Law Rule of Procedure 12.340, [PARTY] submits the following 
interrogatories to [OPPOSING PARTY], to be answered fully, separately, and under oath 
within thirty (30) days of service.

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Each interrogatory must be answered separately and fully in writing under oath.
2. If you cannot answer any interrogatory in full, answer to the extent possible, 
specifying your inability to answer the remainder.
3. These interrogatories are continuing in nature. If information responsive to any 
interrogatory is obtained after your initial response, you must promptly supplement.
4. If any interrogatory is objected to, state the specific grounds for the objection. 
If an interrogatory is only partly objectionable, answer the remainder.
```

### INCOME / FINANCIAL INTERROGATORIES

```
INTERROGATORY NO. 1: Identify all sources of income you have received during the 
period [DATE] through [DATE], including the source, amount, and frequency of each 
income stream (wages, salary, bonuses, commissions, self-employment income, rental 
income, investment income, distributions, draws, or any other form of compensation).

INTERROGATORY NO. 2: Identify all financial accounts (checking, savings, investment, 
retirement, cryptocurrency, or other) held in your name, jointly with any person, or 
over which you have signature authority or control, as of [DATE] and at any time during 
the period [DATE] through [DATE], including the institution name, account number (last 
four digits), account type, and approximate current balance.

INTERROGATORY NO. 3: Identify all businesses in which you hold or have held any 
ownership interest during the marriage, including the name, type of entity, your 
percentage ownership, your role, and the nature of the business.

INTERROGATORY NO. 4: For each business identified in Interrogatory No. 3, state your 
total compensation, draws, distributions, bonuses, and expense reimbursements for each 
of the years [YEAR] through [YEAR].

INTERROGATORY NO. 5: Identify any real property in which you hold or have held an 
ownership interest at any time during the marriage, including the address, the date 
acquired, the acquisition price, the current fair market value, and any mortgage or 
lien on the property.
```

### PARENTING INTERROGATORIES

```

schedule for the minor child(ren), including your proposed primary residence, school 
enrollment, and holiday schedule.

INTERROGATORY NO. [X]: Identify all healthcare providers (physicians, therapists,
```

## REQUESTS FOR ADMISSION — TEMPLATE

**Rule**: Fla. Fam. L.R.P. 12.370  
**Response deadline**: 30 days from service  
**CRITICAL**: Failure to timely respond = matter DEEMED ADMITTED  
**Strategic use**: Pin down positions, authenticate documents, eliminate disputed facts before hearing  

```
[CAPTION]

[PETITIONER'S / RESPONDENT'S] FIRST REQUESTS FOR ADMISSION TO [OPPOSING PARTY]

Pursuant to Florida Family Law Rule of Procedure 12.370, [PARTY] requests that 
[OPPOSING PARTY] admit or deny the following matters within thirty (30) days of 
service of these requests. Failure to respond within the time provided will result 
in each matter being deemed admitted pursuant to Rule 12.370.

Each matter is admitted unless, within the time allowed, you serve a written answer 
or objection. An admission under this rule is conclusively established unless the 
court permits withdrawal or amendment.
```

## STRATEGIC NOTES — RFAs

- Use RFAs to lock in facts the other side cannot credibly deny — document authenticity, financial figures from their own records, dates, amounts, transfers
- Do NOT use RFAs for genuinely contested material facts where the other side will just deny — save those for interrogatories or deposition
- The deemed admission rule is powerful. If the deadline passes without response, file a motion to confirm admissions, not a motion to compel
- Attach the actual documents you are seeking to authenticate as exhibits to the RFA itself — do not reference documents the other side has not seen
- Keep each RFA to one discrete fact — compound RFAs invite valid objections
