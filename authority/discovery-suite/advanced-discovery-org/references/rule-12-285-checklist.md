# Rule 12.285 Mandatory Disclosure Checklist

**Due**: 45 days from service of applicable petition. Continuing duty to supplement when prior information becomes inaccurate.

---

## Financial Affidavit
- [ ] Correct form used: 12.902(b) short form (gross annual income under $50,000) or 12.902(c) long form ($50,000 or more)
- [ ] Signed and notarized
- [ ] Income section complete (all sources listed)
- [ ] Expenses section complete
- [ ] Assets and liabilities complete and dated consistently
- [ ] Consistent with tax returns served

## Tax Returns / Income Documents
- [ ] Federal and state income tax returns (incl. gift and foreign) — past 3 years, all attachments (if most recent year unfiled: W-2s, 1099s, K-1s for that year)
- [ ] W-2s for most recent year
- [ ] All 1099s (interest, dividends, self-employment, rental, other income)
- [ ] For self-employed or business owner: Schedule C, corporate/partnership/LLC returns, K-1s

## Pay Stubs / Proof of Current Income
- [ ] All pay stubs or other evidence of earned income — 6 months preceding compliance
- [ ] Statement of amount and source of all income — 6 months (if not shown on pay stubs)
- [ ] If self-employed: proof of current income (bank statements, client invoices, or accountant letter)

## Bank/Financial Account Statements
- [ ] All accounts (checking, savings, money market, closed accounts) — 12 months, plus canceled checks and registers for check-writing accounts
- [ ] Joint accounts included
- [ ] Business accounts included if self-employed

## Investment/Brokerage/Retirement Statements
- [ ] Brokerage / investment account statements — past 12 months
- [ ] Retirement account statements (IRA, 401(k), pension, deferred comp) — most recent plus past 12 months, plus summary plan description
- [ ] Virtual currency statements — past 12 months, plus listing of all current holdings

## Real Property
- [ ] Deeds for all real property
- [ ] Most recent mortgage statement
- [ ] Most recent real estate tax bill
- [ ] Appraisals (if available)

## Life Insurance
- [ ] Most recent statement for all life insurance policies with cash value

## Other Assets / Liabilities
- [ ] Titles for motor vehicles
- [ ] Loan documents for all outstanding obligations (auto, personal, business)
- [ ] Credit card and charge account statements — past 24 months
- [ ] Loan applications, financial statements, credit reports — past 24 months

## Business Interests
- [ ] Business tax returns (most recent 3 years) if party has any ownership or interest in a corporation, partnership, or trust
- [ ] Corporate, LLC, or partnership agreement
- [ ] Most recent financial statements (profit/loss, balance sheet)

---

## Deficiency Tracking

For each missing item, note:
- Item description
- Whether it was listed on the Certificate of Compliance as "N/A" or just omitted
- Whether it appears in another document produced (check for inconsistencies)
- Whether a deficiency letter has been sent and the date sent
- Whether response to deficiency letter was received

---

## Red Flags That Trigger Advanced Discovery

- Income on FA inconsistent with prior years' tax returns
- Business income dramatically lower than prior years without explanation
- Depositing business income into personal accounts without separate business account
- Assets listed at values inconsistent with public records (especially real estate)
- Retirement accounts listed without statements (just a dollar figure)
- Credit card balances claimed to be "paid monthly" but no statements produced
- FA signed but Certificate of Compliance not filed with court
- Documents produced but not indexed to certificate
