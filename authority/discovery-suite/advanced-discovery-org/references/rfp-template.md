# Request for Production — Template & Issue-Based Request Bank

**Rule**: Fla. Fam. L.R.P. 12.350  
**Response deadline**: 30 days from service  
**ESI**: May specify form of production

## INSTRUCTIONS BLOCK

```
Pursuant to Florida Family Law Rule of Procedure 12.350, [PARTY] requests that 
[OPPOSING PARTY] produce for inspection and copying all documents and things described 
below within thirty (30) days of service of this request, at the offices of [FIRM NAME], 
[ADDRESS].

INSTRUCTIONS:

1. These requests are directed to all documents and things within your possession, 
custody, or control, including documents held by your agents, employees, attorneys, 
accountants, or other representatives.

2. If any request cannot be responded to in full, respond to the extent possible, 
specifying your inability to respond to the remainder and stating all information or 
knowledge you have concerning the portion to which you cannot respond.

3. If any response states an objection, state the grounds completely. If a request is 
only partly objectionable, respond to the remainder.

4. If any document has been destroyed, placed beyond your control, or otherwise 
disposed of, identify the document by: author(s), date, type, subject matter, date of 
destruction, reason for destruction, and person authorizing destruction.

5. Each request should be responded to separately, with documents identified by 
reference to the particular request for which they are produced.

6. These requests are continuing in character and require supplemental responses if 
you obtain different or additional documents prior to and including the date of trial.

7. If any document is withheld on privilege grounds, state with particularity the 
grounds for the claim and describe the nature of the document.

8. For electronically stored information (ESI), produce documents in [specify: 
searchable PDF / native format / Excel natively, not printed to PDF / text message 
thread exports with metadata / QuickBooks backup file]. If no format is specified, 
produce ESI in the format in which it is ordinarily maintained.
```

## DEFINITIONS BLOCK

```
DEFINITIONS:

"Document(s)" means any written, printed, typed, or electronic matter of any kind, 
including all originals, copies, and drafts, and includes but is not limited to: 
contracts, agreements, correspondence, letters, emails, text messages, WhatsApp and 
other messaging app communications, social media posts and messages, bank statements, 
financial statements, tax returns, pay stubs, invoices, receipts, ledgers, QuickBooks 
or other accounting data files, spreadsheets, photographs, video recordings, and any 
electronically stored information on any device or platform.

"Communication(s)" means any transmission of information, whether written, oral, or 
electronic, including emails, text messages, app-based messages, voicemails, and letters.

"Relevant Period" means [DATE] through the present, unless a specific request states 
otherwise.

"You" and "Your" refers to [OPPOSING PARTY] and all agents, employees, attorneys, 
accountants, or persons acting on your behalf.

"Financial Account" means any checking, savings, money market, brokerage, investment, 
retirement, cryptocurrency, or other account at any financial institution or platform.
```

## DRAFTING NOTES

- Always confirm with attorney whether to specify ESI format — particularly important for accounting software, text message exports, and financial institution data
- For cash-intensive business owners, request both personal AND business accounts
- For dissipation requests, be specific about date range and categories — avoid open-ended "all documents" without a time anchor
- If you know specific institutions (from FA or tax returns), name them in the request
- Tag and save the service email in Smokeball under the matter with the "Discovery" tag
