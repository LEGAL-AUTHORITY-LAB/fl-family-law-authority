Limited appearance

One hearing at a time

For existing coaching clients, we can step in for a single, defined event — a case-management conference, a specific motion or hearing — quoted case-by-case for that appearance. It isn't a standalone service you select on your own; we scope and price it with you.

Please note: flat-fee and limited-appearance representation are offered at our discretion and require a paid consultation — we confirm the scope and approve the engagement before either is available.
