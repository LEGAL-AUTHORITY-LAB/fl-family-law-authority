Your Legal Representation Options in Florida Family Law

By Leisa Wintz, Esq. | Family Matters Law Group, P.A.

Not every family law case needs — or can afford — full, hourly-billed representation from start to finish. Family Matters Law Group, P.A. structures representation around four stages of a case (preparation and filing, discovery, negotiation and mediation, and trial and hearings) and offers several ways to get support at each stage.

Full Representation

We handle every aspect of your case, from the first filing through trial if necessary, billed hourly. This is the right fit for complex, contested cases or anyone who wants comprehensive support without managing any part of the process themselves.

Flat Fee Through Mediation

This option covers all services up through and including mediation for one predictable flat fee. If your case settles at mediation — which is common — no further fees are required. This is a cost-effective option for cases likely to resolve without a trial, giving you cost certainty from the outset instead of open-ended hourly billing.

DIY Legal Coaching and Limited-Scope Representation

For clients who want to manage parts of their case independently, we offer staged support, including:

  • Document preparation guidance
  • Discovery support
  • Optional limited-scope representation for a single hearing or mediation session

This flexible model lets you handle what you're comfortable handling yourself while still getting professional guidance exactly where you need it — saving costs compared to full representation.

Not sure which option fits your case? The right fit depends on how contested your case is, your budget, and how comfortable you are handling parts of the process yourself. A consultation is the fastest way to figure out which approach makes sense.

How Billing Works Across These Options

Traditional hourly billing remains standard for full representation, but it can create uncertainty as a case progresses and issues develop. Flat-fee options exist specifically to solve that problem for the portion of cases most likely to settle before trial — giving clients transparency and predictability that hourly billing alone doesn't provide.
