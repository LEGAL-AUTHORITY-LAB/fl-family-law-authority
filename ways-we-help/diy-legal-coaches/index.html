Handle your own case with a lawyer coaching you through it

How it works

You run the case. We make sure you don't run it into a wall.

Plenty of people can handle their own Florida family case — they just shouldn't do it alone. DIY Legal Coaching gives you a licensed attorney to review documents, plan strategy, prep you for hearings, and catch the mistakes that quietly sink pro se cases: bad time-sharing language, missed disclosures, unenforceable terms.

Pay for what you need

Flat fees by service — document review, parenting-plan prep, child-support strategy, MSA review, evidentiary-hearing prep, and more — so you only pay for the help you actually use. For ongoing help, you can pay into a retainer we bill against as you ask us to do things — coaching-style, not full representation. Every price is on the pricing page.

$250 consultation, credited. Your initial consult fee is credited toward any retainer over $2,500. Fees are non-refundable but flexible for upgrades, a conflict check is required before we start, and retainer engagements carry a simple 30-day notice — no fine-print surprises.

Pair it with paralegal drafting

Here's the difference: your documents are built by our attorneys and reviewed by our attorneys — not handed off to an independent paralegal working from a generic template. Our paralegals prepare your documents; coaching teaches you what they mean. Together they're a genuinely affordable way to get a Florida case done right without full representation.
