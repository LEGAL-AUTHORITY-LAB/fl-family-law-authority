Three ways to be represented

Pick the level of representation that fits the case

Not every case needs the same thing. Some need a lawyer on every hearing; some need a fixed price for a known, uncontested outcome; some need a lawyer for one specific hearing and nothing more. We offer all three, and we'll tell you honestly which one your situation actually calls for.

Full Representation

Best for contested cases

We handle everything — filings, discovery, negotiation, hearings, and trial if it comes to that. The right call when the other side has a lawyer, when there's real conflict over money or the kids, or when the facts are complicated.

Retainer-based · see rates

Flat-Fee Representation

Best for known, uncontested outcomes

A single fixed price for matters with a predictable path: uncontested divorce, uncontested modifications, enforcement, prenups and postnups, step-parent adoption, and final-judgment reviews. No hourly meter, no surprise bill.

Flat fee · see pricing

Limited Appearance

Best for one specific hearing

We appear for a single defined event — a case-management conference, an emergency motion, a specific hearing — and step out when it's done. Real representation for the moment that matters, without retaining us for the whole case.

Priced per hearing · see rates

Matters we handle on a flat fee

These are the matters where the path is clear enough to quote a single price up front:

  • Uncontested divorce (with or without children)
  • Uncontested modifications of support or timesharing
  • Enforcement of an existing order
  • Prenuptial and postnuptial agreements
  • Step-parent adoption
  • Final-judgment reviews

Every flat fee lives on one page — our flat-fee pricing page — so you never have to reconcile two different numbers from two different pages.

Truth + context: a lawyer isn't always the cheapest path, and it isn't always the right one. If mediation or DIY coaching fits your case better, we'll say so — see Mediators and DIY Legal Coaches.
